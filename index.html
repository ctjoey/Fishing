Log an order

One order, one line. No account, no email.

    Kept

    Site said In Stock, tracking arrived within 3 business days of the order.

    Phantom

    Site said In Stock, then: cancelled, backordered, partial, or tracking took more than 3 business days. Silence past 3 business days also counts.

    Pending

    In-stock orders under 3 business days old with nothing yet. Not scored either way.

    Public reports

    Incidents quoted in public reviews and forums while researching the ten retailers, with source links. They are context, not part of the rate.
